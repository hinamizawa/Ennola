# Optional computation files

The submission's proofs use written arguments and explicit finite calculations. [verify-finite.m](verify-finite.m) optionally checks the arithmetic retained in the manuscript; [finite-verification.md](finite-verification.md) is the separate explanation for the journal and records its successful final run.

[verify-specialization.m](verify-specialization.m), its [technical notes](specialization-verification.md), and the [development results](specialization-development-results.md) concern examples removed at the author's request. They are preserved as development work and excluded from the submission package. Those earlier integer-discovery attempts did not yield a completed verification. Completed, failed, timed-out and interrupted runs remain distinct in the records.

The subsequently requested [local integer rank-five search](integer-rank-five-search.md) uses new scripts and exact point/residue certificates for `conj:integer-rank-five`. Four parallel instances completed the broad and deeper passes within 90 minutes 23 seconds, certifying 42 distinct parameters, including 34 beyond the prior eleven-element list; both final replays passed. Its [results](../build/integer-rank-five-2026-10-02/RESULTS.md) are separate from the earlier unsuccessful specialization attempts. It supplies exact lower bounds of five for these integer fibres, preserves both manuscript sources and leaves the infinitude conjecture open.

The follow-up [conjecture refinement note](integer-rank-five-refinements.md) compares positive parameters, the additional Pell square condition, congruences and root numbers. It contains proposed formulations and clearly labels the conditional rank-parity inference; neither manuscript has been edited.
