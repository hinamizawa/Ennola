Dear Editors,

Please consider my manuscript, “Prescribed ideal classes and elliptic points in Ennola cubic fields,” for publication in the Canadian Mathematical Bulletin.

The paper uses squareclasses in Ennola cubic fields to connect two explicit constructions: prime ideal classes of prescribed power-of-two order and independent rational points on elliptic curves. For every rational prime at least 5 and every power of two at least 4, it specifies a prime ideal above that rational prime with exactly the required class order. The argument also covers nonmaximal defining orders.

For the associated elliptic curves, the paper proves independence of four points under explicit square and congruence conditions and gives a complete Pell parametrization of those conditions. A genus-one base change produces five independent sections whose generated subgroup is saturated at two, yielding infinitely many geometrically nonisomorphic rational fibres of rank at least five.

The paper develops established unit-squareclass and specialization methods into explicit arithmetic results. Its connection between ideal classes and elliptic points is intended to be accessible to readers in number theory and arithmetic geometry, making the Bulletin a suitable venue for its consideration.

The manuscript is unpublished and is not under consideration elsewhere.
