Dear Editors,

Please consider my manuscript, “Ideal Classes of Order 2^n in a Family of Totally Real Cubic Fields and Rational Points on Associated Elliptic Curves” (arXiv identifier: 2610.04254), for publication in Publicationes Mathematicae Debrecen.

The paper studies ideal classes and rational points through squareclasses in Ennola's family of cubic fields. For every rational prime at least 5 and every power of two at least 4, it constructs a field with a specified prime ideal above that rational prime having exactly the prescribed class order. The proof applies in the maximal order, including cases where the defining monogenic order is nonmaximal.

The same squareclass obstructions give four independent points on associated elliptic curves and, under additional explicit coprimality conditions, two independent ideal classes of order two. A Pell equation describes all parameters satisfying the square and congruence conditions. A further genus-one base change gives five independent sections and infinitely many geometrically nonisomorphic rational fibres of rank at least five.

The arguments develop and apply established results on units, local squareclasses, and specialization. The paper emphasizes explicit constructions and their proofs, and its connection between cubic class groups and elliptic curves is intended for readers interested in algebraic number theory and Diophantine equations.

The manuscript is unpublished and is not under consideration elsewhere.
